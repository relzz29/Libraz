Why we’re here

Every reader deserves a library that gets them.

We believe a great library is more than shelves and search bars. It’s a doorway to new ideas, a quiet place to focus, and an invitation to keep asking questions.

Our mission is to make library access feel natural and welcoming. Libraz brings the essential parts of the experience into one thoughtful digital home, so readers can spend less energy managing their books and more time enjoying them.

What is Libraz?⌄

Libraz is a digital home for your library. Browse the catalog, keep track of borrowing, and make more room in your day for reading.

Do I need an account to browse?⌄

You can explore the catalog without signing in. To access your account and reading activity, log in with the details provided by your library.

How do I borrow or return a book?⌄

Open the catalog to find a title, then follow the borrowing options shown by your library. You can check your active loans and return details in the circulation area.

Can I use Libraz on my phone?⌄

Yes. Libraz is designed to work in your browser on phones, tablets, and computers, so your library is easy to reach on the device you have with you.

Where can I get help signing in?⌄

Start at the login page. If you need an account or have trouble with your credentials, your library team can help you get set up.
